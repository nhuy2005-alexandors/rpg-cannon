# HẮC NHẬT — Emergent World Simulation + Fortune & Misfortune V1.0

**2026-10-22 · Forward-only T346+ · No game turn on installation**

## Purpose
World incidents (broken straps, bad terrain, animals, strangers, bandits) and unlikely windfalls arise causally without the player requesting an event. Events can be good, neutral or bad; no forced karmic balance, guaranteed hits, pity timer, or level-scaled jackpot. This is **one small pure sampler**, not a world daemon or a replacement combat engine.

## Audit decisions and invariants
1. **Sampling unit** is a unique verified real travel segment, significant elapsed time at least 30 minutes, or equipment stress on an already worn load-bearing item. No draw per chat message or turn by default. GM establishes stable opportunity ID and visible evidence tags **before** any draw. A repeat segment in the same turn and kind, even with a new ID, cannot roll again. Stored receipt IDs survive normal checkpoints.
2. **Probability tables** (basis points, sum 10000) per **eligible opportunity**, not every combat action: safe = 80% none, 12% ambient, 6% encounter, 2% hazard; dangerous = 50% none, 20% ambient, 20% encounter, 10% hazard. These are two starter risk bands, not a universal truth about all regions. "Dangerous" must be justified by player-known locale/route evidence; no arbitrary zone switching to chase outcomes.
3. **Catalog and prerequisites:** candidate events have fixed IDs and tag requirements; wear+load for strap failure; rocky/unstable terrain for rock hazard; bandit territory for bandit scout. A category with no eligible incidents becomes none/gated, **without reroll**. GM cannot inject arbitrary lucrative items or blind ambush templates into the public state.
4. **Fortune tiers** only after a qualifying *fortune encounter*: ordinary70%, notable23%, rare6%, exceptional1%. Tier is descriptive of opportunity quality; not a cash payout. A purse found isn't owned until noticed, secured and physical contents verified; even an exceptional tier cannot conjure pre-unknown legendary inventory from thin air.
5. **Lottery** is not autogenerated: only if a previously existing venue, ticket cost, odds, prize/pool and purchase were declared before draw. Jackpot chance is not 1% default. Transactions and claim IDs are separate, idempotent and paid at most once.
6. **Contest randomness** is a separate optional pure draw: before a shot, legal LOS and source-defined hit probability must exist. No approved generic combat accuracy formula exists yet, so optional contest draw *requires explicit approved chance in basis points* and an evidence source; there is no default auto-hit/auto-miss chance. NPC mistakes must be causal, not a quota.
7. **Determinism**: SHA256 versioned, domain-separated, counter-based unbiased rejection sampler. Old V1.3 public seed may be supplied to draw. Its public visibility means future draws are **predictable to an adversarial player**, so this is reproducible not secret. Do not claim cryptographic hidden dice; commit-reveal would be a separately approved extension.
8. **No auto effects**: receipt is PROPOSED_NOT_ADJUDICATED; never automatically mutate HP, equipment, currency, XP, Souls, TH, NPCs, map or quest. The narrative consequence must be physically verified and handled via normal GM user turn plus resource ledger. Only observable public tags in receipts. Hidden NPC details require the existing GM precommit contract.
9. **Authority and storage**: Canonical T345 current state, map, original V1.3 progression remain untouched by implementation. From T346 a GM may call this helper on genuinely eligible opportunities and store receipts in the in-session pending deltas until checkpoint (10-turn or major event), using GitHub readback once. There is no host hook and no unattended ticking.
