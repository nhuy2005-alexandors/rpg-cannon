GROUP AT THE TEMPLE / BASE

The player's base is Điện Tế Huyết in Hắc Nha Thành; the player informally refers to it as “chỗ Tư Tế Xương” or “căn cứ Tư Tế Xương”. Edran is that skeletal priest. The GM failed in T151 to remember this base and then omitted companions in T152–153; those omissions are narration errors, NOT canon death or exile.

EDRAN — TƯ TẾ XƯƠNG

CONFIRMED identity: priest Edran at Điện Tế Huyết, explicitly called “vị tư tế xương” in old scenes T69 and T90. Bone fingers, staff, blue flame in eye sockets, magical bone barriers/restraints.

Function: priest, healer, trainer of Vô Danh, knowledgeable adviser, ally of base. T50 heals; T51–54 trains/helps techniques; T69–70 identifies/interprets Tam Nha sigil. Historical link to Hắc Nha military; previously treated Kharzug before confinement.

Combat: actively helps Maela and Vô Danh in T91–93 versus Truy Liệp Thú; bone constraints.

LAST VERIFIED: safe and alive at Điện Tế Huyết after T93. No supported later death, departure or hostile turn.

UNKNOWN: exact location at T153, private history, additional hidden motives.

MAELA — FEMALE HUNTER

CONFIRMED: “Maela. Thợ săn tự do”, introduced in T69. Initially wary/injured; found Huyết Lang pup beneath burned tree in Rừng Xương and brought it to Edran’s temple, wearing worn black chain. She tried to remove pup’s collar but failed.

Relation: free hunter and combat ally to Vô Danh, not automatically romantic partner or sworn subordinate. T90–93 she uses ranged weapons and coordinates with Edran and Vô Danh against Truy Liệp Thú. Vô Danh shields her during attacks.

LAST VERIFIED: alive and uninjured at Điện Tế Huyết after T93.

UNKNOWN: precise location since, personal history, whether she permanently joined base, explicit relationship status. Do not conjure departure.

KHARZUG — AGED WAR BEAST / “SÓI GIÀ” IN PLAYER REFERENCE

CONFIRMED: named Kharzug T73, ancient eyeless/armored war beast. Was gate keeper “đơn vị canh giữ số III” at Cấm Lao (T75), part of Tam Nha war-beast system under TAM NHA — QUÂN LỆNH 19 (T90).

History: chained into gate’s biological lock; hind leg pinned under rubble, heavy injuries. Vô Danh manipulated/relieved locking load and freed Kharzug while keeping main gate sealed (T88–89). Vô Danh had temporary old military command over unit III; explicitly NOT a pact. Kharzug was brought to Edran for treatment T90.

Pup: shows distinct protective reaction, shields pup from pursuers; pup hides under his body during T91–93. Touches pup’s forehead with metal mask after battle saying “An toàn... rồi”.

LAST VERIFIED: alive and in need of continued recovery at Điện Tế Huyết after T93.

UNKNOWN: actual taxonomy beyond ancient war beast (the player informally calls him old wolf), age, source of armor, full recovery at T153, further travel.

Do not merge him with Huyết Lang Độc Nhãn; that DIFFERENT elite enemy was killed in T66.

HUYẾT LANG NON — SÓI CON (UNNAMED)

CONFIRMED: Huyết Lang pup found by Maela at burnt tree, not traveling with main wolf pack, taken to temple. Wears mysterious collar with three interlocked fangs (T68–70), related to ancient Tam Nha mark. The collar was NOT deliberately activated or bound to Vô Danh.

Relation to Kharzug: approached and hid beneath him, protected by him. The blood scent is said to be different; actual familial relation is UNKNOWN. It is NOT established that Kharzug is biological parent. Maela initially rescued/carried it.

LAST VERIFIED: alive and safe at Điện Tế Huyết after three Truy Liệp Thú were eliminated at T93.

UNKNOWN: name, parents, origin of collar, source of tracking, exact position at T153.

Hostile older one-eyed blood wolf Lv5 of Rừng Xương, encounter T65–66. Killed by Vô Danh using Huyết Trảm Nhị Liên T66. NOT KHARZUG; no family link to pup verified. Keep permanent death in historical log.
