# KHARZUG — AGED WAR BEAST / “SÓI GIÀ” IN PLAYER REFERENCE

CONFIRMED: named Kharzug T73, ancient eyeless/armored war beast. Was gate keeper “đơn vị canh giữ số III” at Cấm Lao (T75), part of Tam Nha war-beast system under TAM NHA — QUÂN LỆNH 19 (T90).

History: chained into gate’s biological lock; hind leg pinned under rubble, heavy injuries. Vô Danh manipulated/relieved locking load and freed Kharzug while keeping main gate sealed (T88–89). Vô Danh had temporary old military command over unit III; explicitly NOT a pact. Kharzug was brought to Edran for treatment T90.

Pup: shows distinct protective reaction, shields pup from pursuers; pup hides under his body during T91–93. Touches pup’s forehead with metal mask after battle saying “An toàn... rồi”.

LAST VERIFIED: alive and in need of continued recovery at Điện Tế Huyết after T93.

UNKNOWN: actual taxonomy beyond ancient war beast (the player informally calls him old wolf), age, source of armor, full recovery at T153, further travel.

Do not merge him with Huyết Lang Độc Nhãn; that DIFFERENT elite enemy was killed in T66.
