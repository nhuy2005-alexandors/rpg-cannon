# T244 — Nhãn Quan Hiệp Chiến / Coordinated Battle Awareness — Training and Skill Contract

**Created BEFORE resolving T244's six assessed training scenarios.** This is public, transparent NPC-training specification, not an alleged secret dice precommit or previously frozen combat ability. Source player request T244: safe rest 2h, continue drills, make a passive reflecting evolving melee/ranged teamwork and character's battle sense. The player already authorized training. Prior T243 4/5 basic drill was insufficient to award a new skill.

## Proposed Common Lv1 passive on earned successful assessment only

- Exact name: **Nhãn Quan Hiệp Chiến** (`NhanQuanHiepChien`)
- Type: **Common · Passive, qualifying situational awareness**, not an active spell or generic Battle IQ stat.
- Preconditions: V is conscious and attending; a genuinely cooperating teammate is close enough that the relevant movement, stance, shot/readiness or audible/visible signal can reasonably be perceived; there is enough visibility/audibility and ordinary time to process a signal. Not Maela-only.
- Effect, when qualified: **ONE relevant observable ally-coordination cue** (e.g., shooting lane open/blocked by V/terrain, ally changing safe firing angle, teammate visibly preparing to cover or falling back) may be explicitly surfaced within V's normal tactical awareness in the GM's turn. This reflects improved attention and recognition of visible team patterns, **not** mind reading or world reveal. Decision and physical execution remain player's normal legal actions; GM must adjudicate new danger and enemy response.
- No guaranteed hit, dodge, parry, defense reduction, free attack/shot/reload/move/reaction/action, flat % accuracy/damage, party-wide stat aura, psychic targeting, omniscience, automatic multi-agent coordination, assumption of an ally's ammunition, or permanent companion recruitment. Failed contact, concealment, lack of signaling or obstructed view may invalidate the trigger. Ally remains autonomous.
- It does not duplicate the existing `PhoiHopKiemNo` (player's own sword-to-crossbow switch penalty) or `NgamBanOnDinh` (player's own movement-to-aim penalty), or reduce those same modifiers. The benefit is **recognized information about actual ally-coordination**, not a free extra action.
- Base new-skill mastery when first earned **Lv1, 0/40 Skill EXP**, no retroactive award from T243 or from the certification drill. Subsequent qualifying effective activations: normal V1.3 +2, verified valid failure +1; bonus +5 only if reproducible public RNG roll actually performed; anti-farming and practice-context evidence apply. **Lv2 effect NOT yet approved**; reaching 40 Skill EXP requires an approved dedicated Lv2 mechanic before upgrade. Possible future Lv2 would improve anticipation under uncertainty, not automatically add +accuracy/party damage.

## T244 examination declared in advance

At start of turn V had STA75/110. Player directed a safe uninterrupted 2h rest first. Canon safe hourly +20 STA capped at110 => 75->95->110 (+35 actual). No automatic HP/BLOOD/TH healing. Maela may use reusable training arrows, which she recovers and cannot be counted as player ammunition. Team drill:
- Warmup/briefing 10 STA. 
- **Six separate 5-STA assessed live-changing dummy/movement/team-cue scenarios**: total 30 STA for six, 40 STA overall and ~90min active; 110 -> 70 if all six completed.
- PASS threshold **at least 5/6**, including successful recognition of moving ally's safe fire lane, safe rear support and an executable retreat cue; no unsafe friendly-fire release. A skipped shot to avoid friendly fire is safe conduct but can still be a FAIL for a scenario's objective if player obstructs the planned attack.
- Test variations: (1) archer ready signal without verbal orders; (2) fake opposing sniper target plus ordinary melee coverage; (3) change of boulder/cover which invalidates expected line of fire; (4) Maela changes flank dynamically; (5) enemy stand-in presses ally to force coordinated cover; (6) retreat signal while line of sight becomes intermittent.
- Each stage is independently adjudicated. **No certification solely for asking or because T243's prior 4/5 passed.** If fewer than5 or an unsafe friendly-fire shot, leave candidate pending, no skill unlock.
- No hostile enemy, no live blood-bearing target, no triggered Hấp Huyết or +7 BLOOD, no combat loot or experience. This file locks the test criteria and prospective mechanical bounds, does not itself grant skill or move game time.

**Novelty:** One lean passive. Do not introduce a Battle IQ numerical attribute, skill tree or more orchestration subsystems in this scope.
